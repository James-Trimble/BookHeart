The first release of BookHeart, an audiobook player for Windows made for listening with a keyboard and a screen reader.

## Downloads

- **BookHeart-0.1.0-Setup.exe**: installs BookHeart with Start menu and desktop shortcuts, and lets you open `.m4b` audiobooks (and, if you choose, other audio files) with it. You can pick the installer's language and where it installs, and install for just you or everyone on the computer.
- **BookHeart-0.1.0-Portable.zip**: nothing to install. Unzip it and run `BookHeart.exe`; your library is kept in a Data folder next to it.

Windows 10 (version 1809) or later, 64-bit. Windows may warn that the app is from an unknown publisher, because it isn't signed yet: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What's in it

- A library of all your books. Add single files, a book split into many files, or a whole folder of books at once.
- Remembers your place, bookmarks and settings for every book, and where its files are.
- Speed from 0.5× to 3× without changing the voice's pitch, volume up to 300%, and a choice of skip amounts. Set defaults for every book in Settings, or give a book its own in Book settings.
- Chapters from `.m4b` files, or one chapter per file for books in parts.
- "Where am I?" tells you the chapter, position and time left.
- Built for NVDA, JAWS and Narrator: everything is announced, every action has a keyboard shortcut (press F1 for the list), and lists work with the arrow keys.
- Media keys work even when the window isn't in front. Light, dark and high-contrast colours.
