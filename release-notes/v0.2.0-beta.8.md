A test version of StoryHeart 0.2.

## What's new

- Books show their real title, author and narrator, read from the details stored inside the audio files. When a file has none, StoryHeart uses its name as before, and a book you've renamed keeps your name.
- You can search the library by author, narrator or series, and sort it by author, with each series kept together.
- Alt+I and the listening screen include the book's author and narrator.
- The Windows media controls show the chapter, the author, the book and its cover picture.
- Updates download in the background while you keep listening. When one is ready, your book pauses and you can choose Install now, or Later to carry on listening and install it when you close StoryHeart.
- When the licence agreement changes, StoryHeart shows it to you once. A portable copy shows it the first time it runs.
- The licence agreement and third-party notices now include TagLib#, the free library StoryHeart uses to read book details.

## Testing

Please check that your books got their proper titles and authors, and that anything you renamed kept your name. The background download is easiest to test with beta.9: when it comes out, keep a book playing and StoryHeart should pause it and ask once the download has finished. Try Later once, then close StoryHeart and check it updated without opening again.
